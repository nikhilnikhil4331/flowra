# Myflo — Toggle Wars Demo Video (screen short + Arena AI prompt)

**Working title:** `Myflo — Track 01 · Test Something Real`
**Duration:** ~40 seconds · **Format:** 16:9 landscape · **Style:** soft, pastel, warm, cinematic product demo
**Audience:** Toggle Wars judges · Team Niknote · Track 01 — Test Something Real ($2,000)

Honest design rule: **no fabricated stats anywhere.** The video shows the *live* experiment state — exposures + conversion events flow from real onboarding users; win probability is explicitly "real data pending", not a fake percentage. If the generator cannot show real screenshots, use stylized schematic panels instead of impersonated dashboards.

---

## 1. Master prompt (paste into Arena AI video generation)

```
Create a 40-second, 16:9 cinematic product demo video for "Myflo" — a soft, warm, mobile-first women's health companion app that runs a live A/B experiment inside it.

STYLE & MOOD: soft pastel pink & lavender palette (bloom/flow aesthetics), gentle warm light, calm and intimate, premium minimal product cinematography. Soft film grain, slow camera drift, ambient-but-minimal motion. No neon, no heavy contrast, no harsh UI chrome. Warm human, thanking-a-user energy. The app brand uses the script wordmark "Myflo" plus soft flower/petal accents.

SCENE SEQUENCE (keep this order):
0. 0–4s — COLD OPEN / TITLE CARD. Centered script wordmark "Myflo" in a soft serif/script font. Tagline below: "Track 01 · Test Something Real". Small badge text: "Toggle Wars Hackathon · Team Niknote". Flowing petals drift across a soft pink-lavender gradient. Cinematic fade-in.
1. 4–9s — THE FLAG. A large, softly glowing interface panel appears — a Flagsmith "multivariate flag". It shows two option cards side by side, keyboard-pointer style:
   - LEFT card — "variant_a" — \"Get Started\" (existing UI)
   - RIGHT card — "variant_b" — \"Start my cycle 🌸\" (warm, personalized CTA)
   Small monospaced text on the panel: "flag: experiment_ab_test". Text label bottom-left: "One flag. Two experiences." Slow pan.
2. 9–17s — THE FUNNEL. A phone/tablet mockup appears in soft focus — the Myflo splash screen — showing the existing CTA and (as a soft-morphing overlay) the warm CTA. A gentle finger tap animates on the CTA. The screen then transitions to the onboarding funnel: pages of the app sliding into view — "Understand Your Cycle" → "Set Your Goals" — with little checkmarks appearing. Caption: "Exposure — real user, drop-in moment."
3. 17–25s — THE METRIC. Close-up on a tappable check-icon and a small event meter. The wordmark "experiment_conversion_metric" types out in monospaced text beside a small journey icon — "goals setup completed". Caption: "Conversion event — real user action."
4. 25–33s — THE EXPERIMENT VIEW (schematic, honest). A softly glowing dashboard panel appears — clearly stylized, NOT a photorealistic screenshot — showing:
   - Header: "Experiment: experiment_ab_test"
   - A two-bar comparison with the caption "Control (existing CTA)" vs "Variant_b (warm CTA)"
   - The win-probabilities/chance-to-win section reads: "Real data pending — accumulating real onboarding completions". A dashed outline reflects the data-pending state, not invented numbers.
   - A small status badge: "Status: Running".
   Caption: "Win probability from real users."
5. 33–38s — SHIP THE WINNER (schematic). The banner expands — a single accent-colored dot becomes "Variant_b wins — roll out" conceptually. Caption: "Ship the winner — toggle the flag, no redeploy".
6. 38–40s — OUTRO. Centered script wordmark "Myflo" again. Bottom text: "Toggle Wars Hackathon · Team Niknote · Track 01". Small badge: "Real experiment · Real users · Real results". Soft fade to gentle pastel.

VERTICAL TEACHING TEXT (small, elegant on-screen captions throughout):
- Do NOT invent any percentages, counts, visitor numbers, lift, or win probability. Show only placeholder states ("Real data pending", "Tracking exposures"). Never render a fabricated result.
- Keep every screen believable as schematic/abstract UI — clearly stylized, not photorealistic dashboard screenshots.
- Reference the audience: Toggle Wars judges — small, confident, not loud.

OUTPUT: 40s, 16:9, cinematic product demo, soft pastel, no fake data, no invented metrics.
```

**Why this is honest (do NOT remove):** The experiment is live but only real users generate real data — the video must NOT show invented "87% chance to win" numbers. It shows the *shape* of what the dashboard will show once real traffic accumulates.
